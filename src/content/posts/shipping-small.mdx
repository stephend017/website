# Shipping Small, Often

Large launches are risky and hard to evaluate.

Instead, ship in short cycles with explicit success criteria:

1. Define one measurable outcome.
2. Ship the smallest change that can move that metric.
3. Observe behavior, then iterate.

Momentum compounds when each update is easy to reason about.
